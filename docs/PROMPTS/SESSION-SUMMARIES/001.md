## Summary

First session. Moved fast through all pre-implementation phases. Blaine came in with clear instincts — keep it simple, freeform text over structured fields, let the AI parse natural language. The key design tension was between structure and simplicity, and we consistently landed on simplicity with just enough structure to support the AI at generation time.

The most interesting design moment was the location discussion. Blaine initially questioned whether locations needed their own entity or were just lore. We talked through the trade-off and landed on keeping locations as a lightweight entity — name and description only — because they serve as consistency anchors even when sparse. This led to a design principle: the system always pulls in locations for grounding, even when the user doesn't explicitly select them.

## Decisions

1. **Multiple worlds** — low cost, hard to retrofit later
2. **Characters and locations** — name + freeform description, no structured fields
3. **No separate texture entity** — world-building texture lives on characters and locations
4. **Lore artifacts** — separate entity with lifecycle (pending/kept/discarded), born from stories, soft delete
5. **Freeform relationships** — character relationships as prose in the description, not a join table
6. **Story-entity join tables** — structural links between stories and the characters/locations they feature (managed implicitly by user selections at generation time)
7. **Location-aware consistency** — system always pulls locations into generation context
8. **MCP is the primary interface** — web UI is for browsing and manual edits, AI does the interesting work
9. **Story generation guide** — UI guidance for users who haven't generated a story yet, fades after first story

## Insights

- The "keep it simple" instinct served well throughout. Every time we considered adding structure (relationship tables, structured character fields, location hierarchies), the simpler version was sufficient because the AI handles natural language well.
- The design audit caught no contradictions — the decisions were internally consistent. The location discussion was the closest thing to a course correction and it happened during design, not after.
- User stories surfaced the collaborative world-building use case (AI as design partner for new worlds) which wasn't explicit in the GENESYS but falls naturally out of the MCP tools.
